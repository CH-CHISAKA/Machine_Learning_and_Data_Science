
# Frequency
This refers to the number of times a given value or outcome appears in the sample space.

_What is probability frequency distribution?_
A collection of the probabilities for each outcome of an event.

_Why do wee need frequency distribution?_
To try and predict future events when the expected value is unattainable.

_What is a frequency distribution table?_
A table matching each distinct outcome in the sample space to its associated frequency.

_How to obtain the probability frequency distribution from the frequency distribution table?_
By dividing every frequency by the size of the sample space.

_The 36 possible outcomes_

Each die has 6 possible results:

$6*6 = 36$

So there are **36 equally likely combinations** such as:

* (1,1) → sum 2
* (1,2) → sum 3
* (2,1) → sum 3
* (6,6) → sum 12

Each individual combination has probability:
$$\frac{1}{36}$$
