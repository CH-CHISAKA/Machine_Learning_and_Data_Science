
# Expected Values 

This is the specific outcome expected to occur when an experiment is executed.


- _Trial_
Observing an event occur and recording the outcome. 
    - Example
        * Flipping a coin and recording the outcome.

- _Experiment_
A collection of one or multiple trials.
    - Example
        * Filliping a coin 20 times and recording the 20 individual outcomes.

- _Experimental Probability_
The probability assigned to an event, based on an experiment conducted.
    - Example
        * Getting heads would equal the number of heads we record over the course of the 20 outcomes, over 20 (the total number of trials).

Expected Value can be _numerical_, _Boolean_, _categorical_ or other depending on the type of the event one is interested in.

**Example 3.2.0** 
For example, in a survey of 300 households 54 had no children, 117 had one child, 72 had two children, 42 had three children, 12 had four children and 3 had five children.
Table 1.1: Probability Distributions for the Number of Children per Household

```sh 
x	|   p(x)
------------
0	|   0.18
1	|   0.39
2	|   0.24
3	|   0.14
4	|   0.04

------------
 Σ p(x) = 1
```
_expected value discrete random variable_

$$
E(X) = \sum_x x\,p(x)
$$

Using the data from table, the mean can be readily computed as:

$$
μ = 0×0.18 + 1× 0.39 + 2× 0.24 + 3×0.14 + 4 ×0.04  + 5 ×0.01 = 1.5
$$

**Example 3.2.1**
If X is a continuous random variable with probability density function f defined on an interval with (possibly infinite) endpoints a and b, then the mean or expected value of X is 

$$
E(X) = \int_a^b x\,f(x)\,dx
$$

_**E(X)**_ is also called the average value of X. It is what we expect to get if we take the average of many values of X obtained in experiments.


**Example 3.2.2**
- Let $X$ have probability density function:
$f(x) = 3x^2$ with domain: [0,1]

_Solution_

The expected value is: 
$$
E(X) = \int_a^b x\,f(x)\,dx
$$

hence, substitution into the formula:

$$
E(X) = \int_0^1 x(3x^2)\,dx
$$

Integration:
$$
E(X) = \left[\frac{3x^4}{4}\right]_0^1
$$

Evaluate the limits:
$$
E(X) = \frac{3(1)^4}{4} - \frac{3(0)^4}{4}
$$

Simplify
$$
E(X) = \frac{3}{4}
$$

Thus, the expected value of $X$ is: $\frac{3}{4}$ / 0.75
